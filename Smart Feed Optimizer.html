📚 References & Data Sources

  1. Water – Mealworm Halloran et al. (2017) — 0.71 m³/kg protein (= 710 L/kg protein), as reported in Bosch et al. (2024), J. Anim. Physiol. Anim. Nutr. Link
  2. Water – SBM Mekonnen & Hoekstra (2010) — The green, blue and grey water footprint of farm animals and animal products, Vol. 1 & 2, Delft. The 4,613 L/kg protein value for soybean meal is widely cited from this work. PDF
  3. CO₂ – Mealworm (6.03) Model assumption from LCA ranges: 1.7 (Bosch et al., 2019) to 20.40 kg CO₂-eq/kg protein (LCA, 2021). 6.03 is an intermediate assumption.
  4. CO₂ – SBM (9.96) Model assumption from literature ranges 9–15 kg CO₂e/kg SBM (land-use change; Carbon Trust, 2010).
  5. Quail – Performance El-Sabahia Poultry Research Station trial (500 birds, 42 days): BW 348–372 g, FCR 1.72–1.82, dressing 80–88%.
  6. Quail – SBM % Toro (2016) and quail nutrition studies: 24% CP recommended for growing quail; 36% SBM is a calculated model value (SBM ~48.5% CP + other ingredients).
  7. Broiler – Performance Ross 308 at 42 d (Biesiada-Drzazga et al., 2011): BW 2229.9 g; FCR ~1.72 (FAO AGRIS); dressing ~75% (Sci. Rep., 2026).
  8. Broiler – SBM % El-Moniary (2005): 23% CP starter diets used 30–45% SBM; calculator uses 32% as mid-range. PDF
  9. Layer – Performance SASSO Silver or similar commercial layer strain at 42 d: BW ~900 g (male), FCR ~1.86; dressing ~70% (estimate).
  10. Layer – SBM % BIS (2007): layer diets 15–17% CP, SBM as primary protein source; 21% SBM is a model estimate for 17% CP.
  11. Duck – Performance Pekin / White Feather at 42 d: BW 3.3–3.6 kg, FCR ~2.03, carcass ~76%.
  12. Duck – SBM % J. Anim. Sci. Biotechnol. (2026): 19.5% CP for commercial meat ducks; 33% SBM is a model estimate.
  13. Mortality Default 5% is typical for commercial poultry operations; editable by user.
